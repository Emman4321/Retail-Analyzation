# Retail Store Sales Analysis
Name: Emmanuel Fatungase

Project Type: Data Cleaning & Visualization

Tools Used: Excel (Pivot Tables, Charts), Google Sheets

Client/Context: Simulated project for EF Digital Services showcasing sales insights from ~1,000 retail transactions

Date Completed: June 2025

Objective:

Analyze a comprehensive retail dataset (~1,000 transactions) to identify key trends in product performance, discount effectiveness, marketing strategy, and regional sales behavior. Clean the dataset, create pivot tables, and generate actionable visualizations and recommendations.

Data Preparation
Cleaned raw sales data with over 50 columns: customer info, transaction details, sales metrics, product categories, and marketing touchpoints.

Standardized date formats and created derived columns for monthly trends and return status.

Ensured consistency in product names and categories.

Key Insights & Findings
1. Discount Strategy
Discount	Total Sales	Average Profit	Total Quantity
5%	$390,032	$483.88	1,018
10%	$374,021	$178.03	1,024
15%	$355,187	$209.56	1,009
20%	$447,396	$51.53	1,183

Takeaway:
Offering 5% discounts strikes the best balance between sales volume and profit. While 20% drives quantity, it slashes profit margins.

2. Return & Payment Behavior
Cash buyers had lower average unit prices ($281.29) but fewer returns.

Online/Mobile payments had higher return rates and unit prices ($554.76 avg).

Takeaway:
Encourage cash or in-person payments for fewer returns; reconsider mobile discounts or enforce stricter return policies online.

3. Top Customer Segment
Segment	Avg. Total Sales
Business	$1,989.34
VIP	$1,927.33
Individual	$1,888.50

Takeaway:
Business customers are the most valuable; Target them with B2B promotions and priority service.

4. Regional & Marketing Strategy
Region	Top Channel	Total Sales
West	Referral	$554,767
North	Email	$458,896
South	Walk-in	$439,776
East	Referral	$481,773

Takeaway:
Referrals work best in the West and East. The South thrives on in-store walk-ins. Email is king in the North. Marketing must be localized.

5. Monthly Trends
Highest Sales: August 2024 ($215K)

Highest Profit: February 2025 ($37K)

Takeaway:
February is ideal for high-margin sales, while August can be optimized for high-volume turnover.

6. Product Performance by Category
Category	Top Sub-Category	Sales ($)
Apparel	Jackets	$42,618
Electronics	Laptops	$287,908
Furniture	Chairs	$159,658
Home Goods	Microwaves	$60,706

Takeaway:
Push laptops and chairs for revenue, and jackets for seasonal apparel boosts.
